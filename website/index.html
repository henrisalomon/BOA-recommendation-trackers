Data gaps, source coverage and calculation methods

PDF pages are one-based positions in the file. Printed page labels are separate. Links use #page=N with bundled, checksum-verified source PDFs; page opening depends on the PDF viewer. “Locator matched” means automated text matching, not human verification.

Outstanding register counts include under implementation, not implemented, unassessed and review-pending records. Implemented, overtaken by events and closed by the Board are distinct states. Annual rates describe the available assessment population; they are not issuance-cohort rates or official organization-wide rates.
