Data gaps, source coverage and calculation methods

The main status uses the latest available BOA follow-up assessment through the selected reporting year. Recommendations issued in that year without a BOA follow-up assessment are labelled “Newly issued”; this does not imply an implementation status. Details show the latest available SG-reported status and the full report history, including later follow-up. SG updates do not replace the main BOA status, and “Closure requested” does not mean the Board has confirmed closure. Implementation rates use eligible BOA assessments only; newly issued recommendations without an assessment are excluded.

Dataset scope: Strategic Heritage Plan recommendations and all their BOA and SG history are excluded from the database, every dashboard view and Excel/CSV exports. This applies to opening and closing balances, newly issued recommendations, assessments, rates, filters and recommendation details. Original source PDFs remain intact. Published totals that include Strategic Heritage Plan recommendations are shown separately from adjusted in-scope totals in the Excel reconciliation.

PDF pages are one-based positions in the file. Recommendation details show PDF page numbers in the link. Printed-page labels and locator-verification flags remain in the downloadable evidence. Citation links open official PDFs on documents.un.org with #page=N and require internet access. Page opening depends on the PDF viewer. Bundled PDF copies are retained for checksum and locator verification; their paths and checksums are listed in the source report index. Citation links do not automatically fall back to bundled copies. “Locator matched” means automated text matching, not human verification.

Outstanding register counts include under implementation, not implemented, newly issued, unassessed and review-pending records. Implemented, overtaken by events and closed by the Board remain distinct source states. The Trends tab combines them as “Implemented/Closed”: bars count transitions into any of these states, while the rate counts all current-year assessments in these states divided by eligible BOA assessments. A repeated closed assessment counts in the rate but is not a new closure transition. Review-pending assessments are excluded. Annual rates describe the available assessment population; they are not issuance-cohort rates or official organization-wide rates.
