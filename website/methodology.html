Data gaps and methods

Reading the status

The Board of Auditors (BOA) verifies recommendations in its follow-up reports. The selected-year status comes from the assessment published in the selected BOA report. A recommendation issued that year reads “Newly issued” until a BOA follow-up report assesses it. The default recommendation view lists recommendations assessed or newly issued in the selected BOA report. The full register also includes earlier recommendations not listed in that report and shows each one’s latest available BOA status as of the selected year, with its assessment year. Where no BOA follow-up assessment is available, it says “No BOA assessment available”. A carried-forward status is a register value, not an assessment in the selected report. Secretary-General (SG) updates are shown separately. They can add progress, priority, responsible entities and target dates, but do not replace the BOA status.

Reading the charts

Opening and closing balances carry forward each recommendation's last observed BOA status. They are calculated register balances, not totals copied from an annual report. “Closing balance” includes recommendations last recorded as Newly issued, Under implementation, Not implemented or Review pending, and those without an observed BOA status; it excludes those last recorded as Implemented, Overtaken by events or another Board closure. The Trends bars show new changes to “Implemented/Closed”. Annual rates use the recommendations assessed in that year's BOA report as their denominator.
