Data gaps, source coverage and calculation methods

The main status and status filter use the latest BOA follow-up assessment in which a recommendation appears through the selected audit period. When the recommendation is first issued and no BOA follow-up status exists, it is “Newly issued” regardless of SG availability. If no BOA follow-up assessment appears in a later available report, the last reported BOA status is carried forward; the card identifies that BOA observation period. SG statuses remain separate source observations and do not replace the BOA status. “Closure requested” does not mean the Board has confirmed closure. Implementation rates use eligible BOA assessments only; newly issued recommendations without an assessment are excluded.

Extraction and review: Recommendation wording, BOA responses and assessments, and SG fields are presented as extracted from the respective reports. Approved HUM review decisions are identified on the details card alongside the original extracted status and the corrected display status. “All peacekeeping missions” is a collective responsible-entity category when the SG uses that wording; it does not imply attribution to each individual mission.

Recommendation history: Details group available BOA and SG entries by audit period, newest first. The latest period opens initially; year buttons jump to and open earlier periods, and Expand all reveals every report entry. Each BOA panel preserves that report’s verification status, Administration response and Board assessment. Each SG panel preserves the reported department wording, status, priority, progress and whichever target-date fields that report contains. Report headings link to a PDF page. Collapsing a period changes only what is visible on screen.

Dataset scope: Strategic Heritage Plan recommendations and all their BOA and SG history are excluded from the database, every dashboard view and Excel/CSV exports. This applies to opening and closing balances, newly issued recommendations, assessments, rates, filters and recommendation details. Original source PDFs remain intact. Published totals that include Strategic Heritage Plan recommendations are shown separately from adjusted in-scope totals in the Excel reconciliation.

Priority: Cards and the shared filter use the latest reported priority in the available history, including later follow-up after the selected reporting year. This classification stays fixed across historical charts. “Not available” means no priority was captured; it does not mean low priority.

PDF pages are one-based positions in the file. Recommendation details show PDF page numbers in the link. Printed-page labels and locator-verification flags remain in the downloadable evidence. Citation links open official PDFs on documents.un.org with #page=N and require internet access. Page opening depends on the PDF viewer. Bundled PDF copies are retained for checksum and locator verification; their paths and checksums are listed in the source report index. Citation links do not automatically fall back to bundled copies. “Locator matched” means automated text matching, not human verification.

Outstanding register counts include under implementation, not implemented, newly issued, unassessed and review-pending records. Implemented, overtaken by events and closed by the Board remain distinct source states. The Trends tab combines them as “Implemented/Closed”: bars count transitions into any of these states, while the rate counts all current-year assessments in these states divided by eligible BOA assessments. A repeated closed assessment counts in the rate but is not a new closure transition. Review-pending assessments are excluded. Annual rates describe the available assessment population; they are not issuance-cohort rates or official organization-wide rates.
