Data gaps and methods

Reading the status

The Board of Auditors (BOA) verifies recommendations in its follow-up reports. The selected-year status comes from that year's BOA assessment. A recommendation issued that year has the status “Newly issued” until a BOA follow-up assessment is available. If an earlier recommendation has no assessment in the selected report, it reads “Not assessed in selected BOA report”; its earlier status remains in the history. Secretary-General (SG) updates are shown separately and do not replace the BOA status.

Reading the charts

Opening and closing balances carry forward each recommendation's last observed BOA status. They are calculated register balances, not totals copied from an annual report. “Closing balance” counts recommendations that have not reached an implemented or closed state in this register. The Trends bars show new changes to “Implemented/Closed”; annual rates use only recommendations assessed in that year's BOA report. A recommendation with no current-year BOA assessment is not counted in that rate.

Status of the data and remaining checks

The yellow notice on the dashboard flags possible AI extraction errors. A few statuses were changed after human review; the original report marks and decisions remain in the review register. Strategic Heritage Plan recommendations are excluded from the dashboard and downloads.
